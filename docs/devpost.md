**Tagline:** Plan a team event people will actually want to come to.

## Inspiration

Team events default to whatever was easiest to book. The people planning them rarely know what the
group would enjoy, and asking usually ends with the loudest opinion.

## What it does

Common Hours collects explicit preferences, proposes outings and in-office activities, handles
locked arrangements and logistics, and keeps share links, feedback and event history. Qloo adds
evidence about how each option fits the group, compared person by person.

## Why Qloo is essential

Qloo gives each option cultural evidence tied to the tastes people actually stated, so a plan can be
compared on more than price and distance.

## How we built it

A deterministic planner with Qloo evidence per person, in TypeScript, Fastify and React.

## Limits

Qloo shows cultural association, not whether anyone will attend or enjoy an event.
